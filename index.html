How it works

A credential that knows what it's worth

1

Issuers attach credit

A course, talk, paper or program states what it's worth: PDH for engineers, PDU for project managers, CEU, or academic credit hours. Attest applies each system's rules for that kind of activity.

2

Learners build one record

Every credential lands in the learner's own record, with credits totaled by type and tracked against each license's renewal cycle, whichever organization issued it.

3

Credit is accepted where it counts

Schools, licensing boards and certification programs receive a verified record and apply the credit toward degrees, renewals and advanced certifications.

One network, three sides

Traditional platforms stop at the certificate. Attest follows the credit to where it's used.

Features

What nobody else connects

Credit translator

Enter an activity once; see its value as PDH, PMI PDUs by Talent Triangle area, CEU and academic credit.

AI agent with approvals

"Amina presented a 2-hour talk and published a paper" becomes two credited records, waiting for one click.
